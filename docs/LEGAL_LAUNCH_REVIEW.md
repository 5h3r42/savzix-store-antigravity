# Legal launch review

The public policy pages now describe the live customer, account, checkout, and payment behaviour. Before a legal launch review is signed off, AITECH INNOVATIONS LTD must confirm the following commercial and operational facts. Do not guess or publish a policy decision until it is approved by the business owner or legal adviser.

## Decisions still required

1. **Delivery timeframe:** state the normal dispatch and delivery estimate for UK orders, including any exceptions.
2. **Cancellation return postage:** confirm whether the customer pays the return postage for a change-of-mind cancellation, and publish that rule before checkout.
3. **Returns address and process:** the approved provisional returns address is AITECH INNOVATIONS LTD, 483 Green Lanes, London N13 4BS, England. Confirm whether a returns authorisation is required and how customers receive the cancellation form or instructions.
4. **Refund operations:** confirm the internal target for processing eligible refunds and how original delivery charges are handled for a full cancellation.
5. **Privacy retention schedule:** approve the retention periods or clear criteria for account records, order records, support messages, and inactive accounts.
6. **International processing:** confirm the actual data-processing locations and safeguards in the Supabase, Stripe, hosting, email, and support-provider agreements.
7. **Transactional email:** verify the order-confirmation and password-recovery sender address, delivery, and support reply process in production.
8. **Cookie changes:** retain a record that the site uses only essential auth/session cookies and cart local storage. Add a consent mechanism before enabling analytics, advertising, or other non-essential cookies.
