# Category Hero Image Prompts

These are the production prompts used for the seven top-level SAVZIX category heroes. Each prompt uses five catalogue product images as identity references and compositing sources.

Shared requirements across every category:

- 1672 × 941 landscape hero composition
- left 45% kept visually quiet for live website copy
- all five products grouped on the right with complete silhouettes and right-edge clearance
- photorealistic premium retail photography
- preserve real packaging, colours, logos, proportions, labels, and multipack presentation
- no embedded headline, added promotional copy, people, hands, or watermark

## Beauty & Skincare

Products: Beauty of Joseon Ginseng Essence Water, ANUA BHA toner, Bio-Oil Natural Skincare Oil, Aveeno Calm + Restore cleanser, and Bourjois Rouge Velvet Ink lipstick.

```text
Create a polished wide beauty studio composition using the five supplied products. Use a luminous ivory-to-blush backdrop, warm travertine plinths, a shallow translucent peach glass disc, and restrained oat and botanical accents. Arrange the complete products in a layered right-side group with clean readable labels. Use soft diffused daylight, elegant warm highlights, realistic contact shadows, and an ivory, beige, peach, and restrained sage palette. Avoid duplicated products, invented packaging, distorted logos, clutter, excessive flowers, or product overlap.
```

## Fragrance

Products: Armani Code, BOSS Bottled Infinite, Tom Ford Black Orchid, ARMAF Club de Nuit Woman, and Adidas Victory League.

```text
Create a cinematic luxury fragrance composition using the five supplied products. Use a midnight-navy to warm-champagne studio, smoked glass arches, dark polished stone plinths, subtle amber mist, and restrained metallic reflections. Place the bottles in an elegant staggered right-side group with one hero bottle slightly forward. Use dramatic soft rim light and champagne highlights. Avoid nightclub neon, excessive flowers, floating bottles, hidden labels, duplicated products, or invented packaging.
```

## Gift Sets

Products: Elizabeth Arden Red Door, Paco Rabanne Olympéa, Dove Beauty Routine, DKNY Be Delicious, and Aveeno Dewy Days Ahead.

```text
Create a celebratory premium gifting scene using the five supplied gift sets. Use a refined cream studio with deep burgundy ribbon, brushed-gold plinths, and subtle folded tissue-paper forms. Arrange the complete boxes in a tiered right-side display, largest sets behind and smaller sets forward. Use warm editorial softbox lighting and a cream, burgundy, and champagne-gold palette. Avoid Christmas-specific motifs, balloons, excessive confetti, bows covering products, duplicated sets, or invented packaging.
```

## Health & Wellness

Products: Deep Heat patch, A&E first-aid travel kit, Always Platinum pads, dental floss sticks, and Masterplast freeze gel.

```text
Create a trustworthy modern wellness composition using the five supplied products. Use a pale blue-white studio with softly curved white platforms, a translucent aqua panel, a folded towel, and one restrained eucalyptus sprig. Keep the arrangement accessible and balanced with visible labels. Use bright diffused daylight for a reassuring, hygienic, non-clinical mood. Avoid hospital staging, pills, syringes, dark shadows, medical claims, clutter, or invented packaging.
```

## Suncare & Travel

Products: Ambre Solaire Kids SPF 50 spray, Beauty of Joseon Relief Sun SPF 50, Piz Buin Tan & Protect oil, Bondi Sands Technocolor foam, and Ambre Solaire aftersun spray.

```text
Create a fresh sun-and-travel studio composition using the five supplied products. ```
```

## Electrical

Products: Braun Series 7 beard trimmer, Carmen hair dryer, Carmen black-and-copper straightener, Colgate 360 Sonic toothbrush, and Duracell battery pack.

```text
Create a refined modern technology composition using the five supplied products. Use a graphite-to-soft-silver studio with matte architectural plinths, one copper accent plane, and subtle precise light lines. Arrange the products dynamically but neatly, with controlled cords and crisp directional light. Preserve the exact Duracell pack artwork and readable DURACELL branding. Avoid sci-fi neon, sparks, floating devices, unsafe electrical effects, invented controls, or altered packaging.
```

## Toiletries

Products: Head & Shoulders menthol shampoo multipack, Lynx Africa antiperspirant multipack, Colgate Extra Clean toothbrush pack, Gillette Simply Venus razor multipack, and Dove Go Fresh deodorant multipack.

```text
Create a bright everyday-care composition using the five supplied multipacks. Use a fresh white bathroom-inspired studio with pale mint glass, light terrazzo platforms, soft water-ripple reflections, and a folded white towel behind the products. Balance pack heights and keep labels visible under clean daylight. Use white, pale mint, soft aqua, and authentic navy packaging accents. Avoid foam over labels, bathroom clutter, split multipacks, duplicated products, wet packaging, or invented packaging.
```
